How we work

From idea to something that works

The method we use on our own products is the same one we apply when we work for other people.

Understand

We narrow down the real problem and drop what doesn't help. We come out with a written scope and a clear definition of done.

Prototype

We quickly build the part that settles the main open question, so decisions are made against something you can hold, not a slide deck.

Build

Development in short iterations, with installable builds from the start and frequent reviews. No surprises at the end.

Ship and maintain

Release, bug tracking and updates. A shipped product is where the work starts, not where it ends.
